# Compliance and Data Privacy FAQ

This document outlines compliance postures, security audits, and data privacy rights handling guidelines.

## 1. Regulatory Frameworks
* **HIPAA Compliance**: The platform supports HIPAA compliance standards. We sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) exclusively for customers on the Enterprise subscription tier. HIPAA data storage is restricted to dedicated isolated databases.
* **GDPR Compliance**:
  * **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)**: A standard DPA is available to all customers in their account portal.
  * **Article 20 Data Portability Request**: Under GDPR Article 20, individuals have the right to request a complete export of their personal data.
  * **Statutory Timeline**: Data portability exports must be compiled and delivered to the customer within the statutory 30-day window.

## 2. Security Audits & Status
* **SOC 2 Type II**: The platform undergoes an annual independent SOC 2 Type II audit. The audit report covers Security, Availability, and Confidentiality. SOC 2 reports are available for download under a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
* **ISO 27001 Questionnaire Handling**: When a customer submits an ISO 27001 or general security compliance questionnaire:
  1. Forward the questionnaire to the Sales Engineering and Compliance Team.
  2. The team responds using pre-approved answers. Turnaround target is 3 business days.
* **Penetration Testing**: Independent penetration tests are conducted biannually. The executive summary of the latest report may be shared with Enterprise clients under an NDA.

## 3. Data Residency Options
* Customers may choose to host their data in several regional zones:
  * United States (East/West)
  * European Union (Frankfurt, Germany)
  * Asia Pacific (Singapore)
* Data residency choices are locked upon registration and cannot be migrated mid-cycle without written approval from the Director of Security Operations.
