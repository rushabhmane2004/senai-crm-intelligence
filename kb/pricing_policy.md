# Pricing and Subscription Policy

This document outlines the standard pricing tiers, billing rules, discounts, and seat adjustment procedures for the Agentic CRM Intelligence Platform.

## 1. Product Subscription Tiers
The platform is offered in four distinct service tiers:
* **Free Tier**: Limited to 3 users and up to 100 contacts. Basic reporting features only.
* **Standard Tier**: $29 per seat/month. Includes advanced filters, basic API access, and email integrations.
* **Pro Tier**: $79 per seat/month. Includes automation triggers, custom webhooks, and core reporting features.
* **Enterprise Tier**: Custom pricing starting at $150 per seat/month. Includes 99.9% uptime SLA, dedicated support, HIPAA BAA compliance, and custom rate limit extensions.

## 2. Discounts and Special Pricing
* **Non-Profit Discount**: We offer a 30% discount on the Standard tier subscription for verified 501(c)(3) non-profit organizations and charitable groups. It is not stackable with other offers.
* **Academic Pricing**: Available upon review. Educational institutions and universities must submit an Academic License Request form detailing course registration numbers. Academic approvals require written verification from the Department Head and are subject to annual audit.

## 3. Mid-Cycle Seat Adjustments (Pro-Rata Billing)
* **Seat Additions**: Mid-cycle seat additions are charged pro-rata for the remaining days of the current billing cycle. The additional charge is computed as:
  `Pro-Rata Charge = (Seat Price * (Days Remaining / Total Days in Cycle))`
  The payment method on file will be charged immediately for seat upgrades.
* **Seat Downgrades**: Downgrades or seat reductions take effect only at the end of the current billing cycle. No pro-rata refunds are issued for mid-cycle seat reductions or cancellations.

## 4. Upgrade and Downgrade Rules
* Customers can upgrade tiers (e.g., Standard to Pro) at any time. Tiers upgrades are calculated pro-rata.
* Downgrading subscription tiers is restricted to the contract renewal date and requires a 30-day written notice to avoid auto-renewal.

## 5. Sales Approval Thresholds
* Sales representatives may issue up to a 15% discount on Standard or Pro annual contracts without secondary approval.
* Discounts between 16% and 30% require approval from the Sales Director.
* Any custom pricing or discounts above 30% are restricted to the Enterprise tier and require CFO or VP of Sales approval.
