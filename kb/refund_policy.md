# Refund and Customer Retention Policy

This document governs subscription refund eligibility, billing exception processes, customer retention playbooks, and chatbot dispute handling rules.

## 1. Standard Refund Eligibility
* **14-Day Money-Back Guarantee**: New subscribers may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of their initial purchase.
* **No Refunds after 14 Days**: By default, no refunds, partial refunds, or credit exchanges are issued after the 14-day window.

## 2. Exception Processes for Service Failures
* Exceptions may be granted for verified system failures, prolonged outages exceeding SLA thresholds (Uptime < 99.0%), or billing errors.
* **Credits vs. Refunds**: When an exception is approved, support agents must prioritize offering future service credits (e.g., one month free) over direct cash refunds to preserve cash flow and customer accounts.

## 3. Churn and Reputation Threat Playbook
* **Churn Threat handling**: If a customer explicitly threatens to cancel their subscription due to service frustrations, support agents must immediately execute the Retention Playbook:
  1. Acknowledge frustration and prioritize issue resolution.
  2. Offer up to a 15% discount on the next billing renewal cycle to extend negotiation.
  3. Escalate account management to the assigned Account Executive and Customer Success Lead.
* **Public Review / Reputation-Risk handling**: If a customer threatens public escalations (e.g., negative reviews on G2, Capterra, Twitter, or Trustpilot), the agent must escalate the case immediately to the CS Lead. Do not auto-reply or promise standard credits.

## 4. Chatbot Misinformation Handling Protocol
If an automated chatbot provides incorrect billing or product information to a customer (e.g., promising a pro-rata refund in violation of standard policy):
* **Protocol Steps**:
  1. **Acknowledge Discrepancy Neutrally**: Support agents must acknowledge the misinformation provided by the chatbot without admitting legal liability (e.g., "I see the virtual assistant provided information regarding refunds that does not align with our terms...").
  2. **Escalate**: The case must be routed directly to the Support Lead.
  3. **Provide Policy Grounding**: Provide the customer with the policy-grounded explanation: "Our standard policy is that all sales are final after 14 days. However, due to the conflicting information from our assistant, we will review this with management."
  4. Never state "we are legally responsible for our chatbot's statements" or similar admissions.
